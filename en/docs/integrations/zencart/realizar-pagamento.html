Payment of the purchase ZenCart

When the customer wants to proceed with the purchase of a product/service, he/she must log in to the Zen Cart shop.

Add items to cart.

To confirm the purchase, click on ‘Checkout’.

Enter delivery details.

Select the PayPay payment method: ‘Credit/Debit Card’ or ATM (‘Multibanco’).

When selecting the ATM option (‘Multibanco’), the purchase summary will be displayed. To confirm the order, click on ‘Confirm Order’.

After this step, the payment reference will be generated.

Once the order has been paid, a success message will appear and the order status will be updated.

If the customer has selected the ‘Credit/Debit Card’ payment option, he/she should click on the link and will be automatically redirected to the PayPay payment area.

Enter your credit card details and complete the payment.

And if they are correct, your purchase is validated.

When accessing the Zen Cart shop you will be able to confirm that the status of your order has changed.
