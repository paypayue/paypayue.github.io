Once the zipped folder has been downloaded, extract the zip files. Then, follow the steps
below for each module you want to install.

![conteudo zip prestashop](../../../../../../_media/prestashop-1.png)

In the administration/backoffice environment of your e-commerce shop, go to the tab ‘Modules’> ‘Modules and Services’.

![configuracao1](../../../../../../_media/prestashop-2.png)

Click on the option ‘Send a module’.

![configuracao2](../../../../../../_media/prestashop-3.png)

The following area will appear where you can drag or select the module to import. The module must be in ZIP or tarball format and you must load only one module at a time.

![configuracao3](../../../../../../_media/prestashop-4.png)

After the previous step, the module is automatically installed in your shop. Click on
‘Configure’ to start its configuration.

![configuracao4](../../../../../../_media/prestashop-5.png)

In the form you must enter the access data to the PayPay webservice. To obtain this data, go to PayPay in Configure > Integrations > Integration Platforms > Add (PrestaShop extension), and at the end, select the option ‘Save’.

![configuracao5](../../../../../../_media/prestashop-6.png)

:::info

Your shop can now generate and provide payment data to your customers, in real time, according to the chosen payment method (ATM, credit/debit card and MB WAY).<br/>
If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are at your disposal to help you with the configuration.

:::
