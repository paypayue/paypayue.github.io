All payments are automatically processed. However, if there is a situation where the payment has been made and the status of the purchase has not been updated in the OpenCart shop, the shop administrator can use the ‘Verify Payments’ button to manually update all pending payments, available in the ‘Payments’ of the ‘Dashboard’.<br/> Also change the ‘Status’ of the payment.
