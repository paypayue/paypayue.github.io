# Payment of the purchase

To purchase a product/service, the customer must log in to the WooCommerce shop.

![compra1](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-7.png)

Add items to cart.

![compra2](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-8.png)

To confirm your purchase, please click on ‘Checkout’.

![compra3](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-9.png)

Once you have filled in the delivery details, you must select the type of payment (ATM - Multibanco-, Credit/Debit Card or MB WAY).

![compra4](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-10.png)

:::info

The configuration of the payment methods must be defined in the PayPay customer area. You can also define the minimum and maximum amounts for the different payment methods to have more control over the limits.

:::

By selecting the ‘Multibanco’ (ATM) option, a payment reference will be generated for the exact amount of the order. In this area you will be able to see a summary of your purchase.

![compra5](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-11.png)

By accessing the order history, you will be able to view the details of the order you have
just placed.

![compra6](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-12.png)

In the details of the last order you placed, you will see the payment reference generated by Paypay.

![compra7](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-13.png)

Once the payment has been made, a success message will appear and the status of the
order will be updated.

![compra8](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-14.png)

In case the customer has selected the ‘Credit/Debit Card’ payment option, he/she will be redirected to the PayPay payment area. You will be asked to enter your credit card details and validate the payment.

![compra9](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-15.png)

By accessing the WooCommerce shop, you can confirm that the status of your order has been duly updated.

![compra10](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-16.png)

:::info

Your shop will send the payment details to customers by email.

:::

If you pay using the ‘MB WAY’ option, you will be redirected to the PayPay payment area.

![compra11](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-17.png)

Enter the mobile phone number associated with your MB WAY account.

![compra12](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-18.png)

After these steps, a payment alert will be sent to the associated number. In the application, authorise the payment and enter the MB WAY PIN. Once the payment has been made, click on the ‘Update’ option.

![compra13](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-19.png)

If the payment is successful, you will see the message shown below. By clicking on ‘Back’, you will be redirected to your WooCommerce shop. To obtain the proof of payment, click on the available option.

![compra14](../../../../../_media/woocomerce-20.png)
